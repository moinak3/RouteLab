## Title

RouteLab in 90 Seconds: Model Routing for AI Agents

## Description

One model is not the best fit for every task an AI agent performs. But deciding which tasks should move to which models, proving the change is safe, and maintaining that policy in production is still difficult.

RouteLab turns production traces and human-calibrated evaluations into an inspectable model-allocation policy. See how teams discover recurring Task Groups, compare candidate models across quality, latency, and cost, publish routing decisions, and keep them calibrated over time.

Learn more and join the RouteLab private preview on the RouteLab website.
