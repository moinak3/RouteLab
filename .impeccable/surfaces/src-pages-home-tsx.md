Audience: AI platform engineers and product teams deciding which model should handle each recurring agent task. The job is to understand RouteLab's evidence-backed allocation loop, enter the preview, and then inspect the same logic in an operational workspace.

## Direction contract

THESIS: RouteLab is a living model-review dossier, not another neon AI dashboard. It makes the decision record itself the visual center: evidence, thresholds, approvals, and version history read as one inspectable artifact.

OWN-WORLD: Warm archival ivory is the ground; navy ink and cobalt rules structure the record; vermilion marks consequential decisions; chartreuse appears only for current or passing states. Editorial serif headlines carry judgment, Inter carries interface language, and DM Mono is reserved for identifiers and measurements. Thin rules, folios, stamps, ledger rows, and revision marks replace floating cards and glows.

The primary action is to enter the private preview.
